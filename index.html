( 01 ) Research

Military systems are only as credible as the tests they survive. Q Labs builds the offensive and defensive cyber models that make those tests rigorous and repeatable.

01

Offensive cyber modeling

Models of attacker tactics and offensive cyber capabilities, used to stress military systems the way a capable opponent would.

02

Defensive cyber modeling

Models of detection, response and hardening, used to measure how well a defense holds and how quickly a system recovers.

03

Simulation environments

Isolated digital ranges that reproduce networks, platforms and mission systems without ever touching live infrastructure.

04

Evaluation & assurance

Repeatable campaigns that measure how offense and defense perform against each other, reported in formats that procurement and certification teams can audit.

( 02 ) Offense

Offense

Attacker behavior, modeled with discipline. We build the offensive side of the test so a system meets a capable adversary in simulation first.

  • Attack-path and campaign modeling
  • Adversary behavior emulation
  • Stress scenarios for mission-critical platforms

( 02 ) Defense

Defense

Resilience, measured rather than assumed. We model the defensive side to show what is detected, how fast it responds and what survives.

  • Detection and response performance
  • Hardening and architecture resilience
  • Recovery and mission-continuity metrics
